STEP 9 - ONE PAIR OF BRACKETS

('or', ('not', ('implies', True, True)), ('and', False, ('not', True)))

Value: False



The operators that made the difference were not and implies because not has a higher precedence than implies. 
In the expression without brackets, not was evaluated first, but when I added the brackets around (True implies True), the implication inside the brackets was evaluated first. 
Because of this, the answer changed from True to False.
